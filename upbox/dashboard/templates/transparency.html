1. Purpose

upbox is a deployer-side network observer. It records the requests that AI tools on this machine make to their cloud services, replaces secrets before forwarding, and keeps a tamper-evident audit log so you can see and control what left the machine. This page is not legal advice.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • A copy of the records: upbox export --format audit -o records.ndjson, or upbox report --records records.ndjson for this page as a file plus the records.
  • Erasure of specific records: upbox erase --id N --reason "...". Erasures are disclosed above and in every later export.
  • Shorter retention: ~/.upbox/rules/retention.yaml.
  • Storing less in the first place: bodies: false and headers: false in ~/.upbox/rules/capture.yaml.

8. Limitations

  • upbox records only traffic that passed through its proxy from a process it was configured to capture. Absence of a record is not evidence that no request was made.
  • A stored body may contain personal data about other people. upbox cannot separate their data from yours.
  • Timestamps are the capture host's wall clock, not attested by any time authority.
  • Erasures are disclosed, not hidden: a tombstone keeps its position and hash so the log stays verifiable.
